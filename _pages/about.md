<p>
I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics at the University of Kentucky. 
</p>

<p>
I did my postdoc at the University of Waterloo (2023-2024) with <a href="https://aukosh.github.io/" style="text-decoration: none;">Aukosh Jagannath</a>. Prior to that, I worked as a Quantitative Analyst at Wells Fargo Bank (2022-2023). I received my B.S. from Sichuan University (2012-2016) and Ph.D. in Mathematics from the University of Utah (2017-2022). Both <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1HKI0I67_Oc&list=RD1HKI0I67_Oc&start_radio=1" style="text-decoration: none;">Chengdu</a> and <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aI2O73pTrpM" style="text-decoration: none;">Salt Lake City</a> hold many sweet memories from my early adulthood.
</p>

<p>
My research interests lie in the mathematics of data. Specifically, I work on random sampling (randomized least squares and algorithmic stochastic localization), ranking, and multifidelity methods. As a problem-driven researcher, I enjoy learning new ideas and am open to potential collaborations. 
</p>
