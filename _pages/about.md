<p>
I joined the Department of Mathematics at the University of Kentucky as an Assistant Professor in 2024. 
</p>

<p>
I spent one year as a postdoc in the Department of Statistics and Actuarial Science at University of Waterloo (2023-2024). Prior to that, I worked as a Quantitative Analyst at Wells Fargo Bank (2022-2023). I received my B.S. from Sichuan University (2012-2016) and Ph.D. in Mathematics from the University of Utah (2017-2022). Both <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1HKI0I67_Oc&list=RD1HKI0I67_Oc&start_radio=1" style="text-decoration: none;">Chengdu</a> and <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aI2O73pTrpM" style="text-decoration: none;">Salt Lake City</a> hold many sweet memories from my early adulthood.
</p>

<p>
My research interests lie in the mathematics of data. Specifically, I work on ranking, randomized approximation, and multifidelity methods. I am supported in part by NSF grant no. DMS-2607989.
</p>
